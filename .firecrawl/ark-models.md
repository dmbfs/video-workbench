Tip​

- Region availability:​

- Currently, all the models listed in [Model list](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ModelArk/1330310) are supported in the ap-southeast-1 region.​

- The seed-2-0 and seedream-5-0-lite models are also supported in the eu-west-1 region.​

- Base URL by region:​

- ap-southeast-1: https://ark.ap-southeast.bytepluses.com/api/v3​

- eu-west-1: https://ark.eu-west.bytepluses.com/api/v3​

For more information, see [Region availability](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ModelArk/2191806).​

All rate limits listed in this document are theoretical maximum values which are not guaranteed, and will be affected by platform load and invocation method. For details, see [Best practices for handling burst traffic](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ModelArk/1848593).​
